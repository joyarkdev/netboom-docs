# Netboom Payment, Subscription and Virtual Items Agreement



## 1. Acceptance and scope

This Payment, Subscription and Virtual Items Agreement (the “Payment Agreement”) governs purchases made through Netboom, including subscriptions, Coins, game time, time cards, promotional purchase offers and other paid digital items or benefits (collectively, “Paid Products”).

Before completing a purchase, please review the product name, price, billing period, included benefits, applicable limits, renewal terms and any trial or promotional conditions shown on the purchase page. By tapping **Subscribe**, **Purchase**, **Continue** or another purchase confirmation button, you confirm that:

1. you have reviewed and accepted this Payment Agreement;
2. the purchase information displayed to you is correct; and
3. you authorize the applicable app store, payment provider or Netboom, as applicable, to charge the selected payment method.

If you do not agree to this Payment Agreement, do not complete the purchase.

## 2. Paid Products

### 2.1 Subscriptions

A subscription provides the benefits displayed for the selected plan during the applicable subscription period. Benefits may include a specified amount of membership game time, queue priority or other features shown before purchase. Different plans may contain different benefits and limits.

Unless clearly identified as non-renewing, a subscription automatically renews for successive billing periods until canceled. The purchase page and the app store checkout page will display the applicable price and billing period before you confirm payment.

### 2.2 Coins and other virtual items

Coins are virtual items that may be used only for eligible features within Netboom, such as exchanging Coins for game time at the rate displayed in the app. Other Paid Products may include game time, time cards or limited digital benefits described on the applicable purchase page.

Coins and other virtual items:

- have no cash value and are not legal tender;
- cannot be redeemed for cash;
- cannot be sold or transferred between accounts unless Netboom expressly provides such a feature; and
- may be used only within Netboom and only for the purposes displayed in the app.

Purchased Coins are separate from promotional or rewarded Coins. Promotional or rewarded Coins may be subject to additional eligibility, usage or expiration rules disclosed with the applicable promotion.

## 3. Prices, taxes and payment processing

Prices are shown before purchase and may vary by product, platform, country or region, currency, tax treatment and promotional eligibility. Applicable taxes or payment charges may be added where required and will be shown by the payment provider when available.

Purchases made through Google Play, the Apple App Store or another third-party platform are also governed by that platform’s payment terms. The price and currency shown in the final platform checkout interface control the transaction.

Netboom may change prices for future purchases. A price change will not alter a completed one-time purchase. For a renewing subscription, any price change will be handled with the notice or consent required by the applicable platform and law.

## 4. Trials and promotional subscription offers

A trial or promotional subscription is available only to users who satisfy the eligibility conditions displayed for that offer. The offer page will state the trial or promotional period, promotional price, included benefits and the regular subscription price that applies afterward.

Unless canceled before the trial or promotional period ends, the subscription will automatically convert to the regular paid subscription and renew at the price and billing period disclosed before purchase.

Eligibility may be limited by account, payment profile, device, prior subscription history, country or region, or other conditions disclosed with the offer. Attempting to obtain the same introductory offer through multiple accounts or other abusive means may result in loss of the offer or other action permitted under the User Agreement.

## 5. Automatic renewal and cancellation

The applicable payment provider will charge the subscription price at the start of each renewal period unless the subscription is canceled before renewal.

You may cancel an active subscription through the subscription-management page of the app store or payment provider used for the purchase. To reduce the risk of an unintended renewal, **we recommend canceling at least 24 hours before the next billing date**. The exact cancellation deadline and renewal processing time are determined by the applicable payment provider and the information shown in your subscription-management page.

Deleting Netboom, signing out, deleting a device profile or not using the service does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Unless applicable law or the platform’s rules provide otherwise, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. You may continue to use the paid subscription benefits until that period ends. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not by itself refund the current billing period.

## 6. Delivery and use of Paid Products

A Paid Product is considered delivered when the relevant subscription entitlement, Coins, game time or other digital benefit is credited to the purchasing Netboom account or otherwise made available for use.

Please confirm that you are signed in to the intended Netboom account before purchasing. Paid Products are normally linked to the account through which they were purchased and may not be moved to another account.

If a payment is completed but the Paid Product is not delivered within a reasonable time, contact customer support with the account identifier, order number, purchase time, product and payment platform. Netboom may verify the transaction with the payment provider before restoring the item or providing another remedy.

## 7. Refunds

Once purchased Coins or other virtual currency has been credited to the user’s Netboom account, it is deemed delivered and is non-refundable, except where required by applicable law.

## 8. Unauthorized and mistaken purchases

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Netboom account, app-store account, payment method and device. Use the purchase authentication and parental-control features offered by your device or payment provider.

If you claim that a purchase was unauthorized or that the payment card or other payment method was stolen or misused, you must report it promptly through the payment provider’s official process and provide Netboom with reasonable and verifiable supporting evidence upon request. Such evidence may include the platform order number, payment record, payment-provider dispute reference, proof of account or payment-method ownership, or a bank, payment-provider or law-enforcement report, where applicable. Do not provide full card numbers, passwords or unrelated personal information.

If you do not provide reasonable supporting evidence sufficient to verify the claim, Netboom may reject the request as unsubstantiated and handle it as a suspected false or abusive refund claim, subject to applicable law and the rules of the relevant payment platform. Knowingly submitting false information, fabricated evidence or a fraudulent refund or chargeback claim is prohibited and may result in reasonable account or payment restrictions permitted under the User Agreement and applicable law.

If this Payment Agreement conflicts with the specific product, price, billing or cancellation information shown in the final purchase interface, the specific purchase information controls for that transaction. Mandatory applicable law and the payment platform’s governing terms take precedence where they cannot legally be excluded.

Netboom may update this Payment Agreement to reflect changes to Paid Products, payment methods, platform requirements or applicable law. Material changes will be communicated as required by law. Changes do not retroactively alter a completed one-time purchase, and subscription changes will be handled in accordance with applicable law and platform requirements.
